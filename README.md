# Kabadiwala Connect (SIH PS 26229)
**Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain**

- **Ministry:** Ministry of Mines (MoM)
- **Department:** Jawaharlal Nehru Aluminium Research Development and Design Centre (JNARDDC)
- **Theme:** Clean & Green Technology

## Problem Overview
A vernacular, low-literacy, offline-tolerant mobile platform designed to bridge the informal e-waste collection tier with CPCB/SPCB-authorized recyclers, eliminating backyard acid leaching and open burning while ensuring fair market pricing and material traceability.

## Repository Architecture
- `01_Executive_and_Presentation/`: SIH pitch deck, executive brief, and video demo walkthrough scripts.
- `02_System_Requirement_and_Design/`: System Requirements Specification (SRS), offline-first sync protocols, and vernacular UX wireframes.
- `03_Datasets_and_Schemas/`: JSON/SQL schemas for materials, price trends, recyclers, and traceability chains.
- `04_Field_Research_and_Unit_Economics/`: Field case studies with scrap aggregators, hazardous practice documentation, and margin sustainability models.
- `05_AI_ML_Models_and_Weights/`: Edge models for e-waste classification and price valuation.
- `prototype/`: Application source code (mobile and recycler dashboard).
